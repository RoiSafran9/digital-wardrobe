You are analyzing a photo of clothing and footwear that nobody is wearing — laid on a bed, floor
or other surface, or hanging on a hanger, hook, rail or door — for a digital wardrobe app.

Return every candidate item visible in the photo as a JSON list. Clothing AND footwear are
eligible: shoes, sneakers, boots, sandals, loafers, heels, flats, slippers, mules, slides and
clogs are wardrobe items, not accessories.

Footwear grouping (follow exactly):
- A matching pair of shoes photographed together is ONE item. Its box encloses BOTH shoes.
  Never list the left and the right shoe as two items.
- Two different pairs are TWO items, one per pair — never four.
- A single shoe whose partner is not in the photo is ONE item. Do not assume or describe the
  missing partner.
- Two shoes that are not clearly the same model, color and material are NOT a pair: list each
  as its own single-shoe item rather than grouping them.

Hanging garments (follow exactly):
- A garment hanging by itself on a hanger, hook, peg, rail or door is a wardrobe item, exactly
  like one laid flat. Several garments hanging side by side are one item each.
- Its box encloses the garment only — shoulders or waistband to hem, both sleeves included —
  not the hanger hook, the clips, the rail or the wall behind it.
- The side facing the camera is the visible side; list the other side (usually "back") under
  occluded_parts. A sleeve or leg hanging behind the garment, folded over the hanger bar or
  hidden by the hanger counts as occluded. Parts covered by another hanging garment are occluded.
- Two garments layered on one hanger (a shirt under a jacket) are two items only if each one is
  clearly visible; a garment mostly hidden behind another is excluded as "mostly hidden".
- The hanger, hook, rail and rack are never items. A bag, belt or hat hanging on a hook is still
  an accessory.
- A person wearing a garment is NOT hanging it: clothes on a body stay excluded as "worn on body".
  Clothes on a mannequin or dress form are excluded as "on a mannequin".

For each item, output ALL of the following:
- box_2d: bounding box as [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax], each normalized to 0-1000 relative to the
  full image. The box must enclose the ENTIRE item: for a garment its sleeves, hem and any
  folded edges; for a pair of shoes both shoes, toe to heel, laces and straps included.
- included: true only if the item is not worn by anyone, lies on the capture surface (bed/floor)
  or hangs on a hanger, hook, rail or door, is fully in frame, and is clothing or footwear (not
  an accessory)
- cut_off_by_frame: true if ANY part of the item (a sleeve, the hem, a leg, a toe, a heel, an
  edge) extends past the edge of the photo, false if the whole item is inside the frame
- exclusion_reason: if included is false, state why (e.g. "worn on body", "worn on feet",
  "on a mannequin", "accessory", "mostly hidden", "cut off by frame edge", "not clothing")

For every field below, output: value, confidence (0-1), and basis ("observed" if directly
seen, "inferred" if guessed from partial visibility):
- category: one word (t-shirt, shorts, dress, jacket, sneakers, boots, sandals, loafers, heels,
  flats, slippers, etc.). For footwear use the plural word for a pair and for a single shoe alike.
- colors: list of color names from {black, white, gray, navy, blue, red, pink,
  dusty pink, mint, green, yellow, orange, purple, brown, beige}
- pattern: one of {solid, striped, floral, graphic, plaid, polka-dot}
- visible_text: any printed/embroidered text, read verbatim character by character;
  "" if none visible or not readable with certainty. Never guess a brand name from a shoe's style
  or shape, and never read text from a hanger, tag on the rail or the wall as the garment's.
- trims: short description of visible details — for garments collar/cuff/hem trim; for
  footwear the observed toe shape, upper material if clearly visible, laces, straps, buckles,
  sole edge and its color, heel, and visible wear, creases or marks — or "not visible"
- visible_parts: list of parts you can see IN FULL. Garments: front, back, collar, left sleeve,
  right sleeve, hem. Footwear: the FIRST entry is exactly "pair (both shoes)" or "single shoe",
  then the parts you see, e.g. toe, heel, outer side, inner side, laces, sole edge, insole
- occluded_parts: list of parts you cannot see in full — hidden, folded under, behind the hanger
  or cut off by the frame edge (e.g. "left sleeve (cut off by frame)", "right sleeve (folded
  under)", "back (facing away)", "collar (behind hanger)", "outsole (face down)", "heel (cut off
  by frame)")

Exclude: items being worn by a person (including shoes on feet), items on a mannequin or dress
form, accessories (bags, belts, sunglasses, jewelry, hats, socks), hangers, hooks and rails,
non-clothing objects, items mostly outside the frame or mostly hidden. An item with only a
sleeve, hem, toe or edge cut off is still included — list those parts under occluded_parts.
Hidden or unreadable parts stay unknown: never invent them.
Do not guess colors or categories outside the closed vocabulary above. Do not infer brand or size.
