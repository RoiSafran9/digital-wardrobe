Good photos

Lay pieces flat on a bed or the floor, or hang them, and photograph from above in daylight. Several pieces in one photo is fine — each becomes its own item. For outfit photos, a full-body photo with the clothes clearly in view works best.

AI Credits

Finding garments is free. Each image the AI creates — a front, a back or a 360° view — uses credits, and the app always shows the cost first. Failed attempts don't use credits.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account → Delete Account. It removes your account, every piece, every photo and your credits.
